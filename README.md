<a id="overview"></a>

**Buffer** is a powerful Firefox add-on designed for seamless sharing of web content across multiple social networks. This innovative tool allows you to share links and schedule posts on platforms like Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn, and App.net directly from your browser. The **latest version** of Buffer enhances your productivity by allowing you to publish content on several profiles simultaneously, making social media management a breeze.
